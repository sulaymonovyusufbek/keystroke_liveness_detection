## What went wrong (short)

The launcher was a bash `for` loop. A non-interactive bash treats a foreground child paused with SIGSTOP as finished
(`exit=147` = 128 + SIGSTOP in the log). Pausing D4 therefore started E5, and pausing E5 started E6. Details and the
fix: `docs/phase3b_bugfixes.md`. The new launcher `scripts/run_phase3b.py` waits through pauses, runs one part at a
time, uses a launcher lock and a training lock, and skips COMPLETE runs.
